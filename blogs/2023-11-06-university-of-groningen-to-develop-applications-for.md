SynSense and CogniGron / University of Groningen are today announcing a collaboration to evaluate the low-power Neuromorphic sensory processors from SynSense. The CogniGron research groups will use the Speck™ low-power vision system to develop visually guided closed-loop systems. They will also evaluate the Xylo™ microWatt Audio processing platform to explore auditory scene analysis applications.
